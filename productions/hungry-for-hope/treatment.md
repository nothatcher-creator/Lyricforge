# Hungry For Hope — #WEED_SMOK

Song date supplied: September 22, 2024. User confirmed production start and requested generated assets, song-specific typography, beat-driven text, and full creative control.

## Direction

Isolation, fracture, persistence. Storm-blue charcoal gives way to silver and a narrow amber light. Three original generated environment plates form a coherent cinematic world: a rain-dark coastal road, a ruined cathedral with a distant aperture of light, and a fractured circular opening facing dawn. No generated performance footage or invented person is presented as the artist.

Both reattached portrait references are incorporated into an additional generated photographic composite for the intro, quiet passages and outro. Source poses are retained; no invented singing performance is claimed.

## Typography and movement

Anton for heavy condensed hook declarations; Oswald for verse phrases. Each phrase has a readable resting state. Word reveals follow vocal cues, measured bass and spectral-flux impacts drive damped scale pulses, selected words get a controlled kick, and chorus declarations occupy more of the frame. Verses use restrained mask reveals; the darker bridge contracts and suspends the text; the final hook expands toward amber light.

The generated plates move through slow opposing camera pushes with foreground parallax, crossfades and deterministic rain/ash/dust. Their motion is procedural animation of images, not generated video footage.

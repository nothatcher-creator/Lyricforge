A full-length cinematic lyric-video production for **DEMONS** by **SWAN**.

- **Video:** 1920 × 1080, 30 FPS, H.264 MP4; complete original stereo AAC stream copied unchanged.
- **Production:** 12 environment plates, 43 timed camera setups, animated phrase typography, music-responsive atmosphere and procedural 3D fragments.
- **Lyrics:** 285 supplied words across 73 timed cues; both solos have instrumental visuals.
- **Files:** full MP4, short preview, editable production bundle, SRT/LRC, and audio-verification report.
- **LyricForge:** import `productions/demons/DEMONS.lyricforge.json` after pulling the repository; included media lives in `public/assets/demons-*`.

Music and Video by Carl Korytko  
Lyrics and Spoken Word by #WEED_SMOK

See [production source and instructions](https://github.com/nothatcher-creator/Lyricforge/tree/main/productions/demons).

The environment treatment is a custom animated 2.5D composite with procedural 3D elements. Heavy accompaniment masks several sung words; `review_note` entries in the supplied timeline identify the cues needing the songwriter's listening review. Text coverage and automated alignment do not establish frame-perfect accuracy for every vocal. This production cut is technically verified, with those timing limits documented.
